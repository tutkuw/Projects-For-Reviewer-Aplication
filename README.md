
I put together this quick macropad PCB in KiCad as a hardware design sample. It pairs a bottom-mounted ESP32 with 9 Cherry MX switches, a bare EC11 rotary encoder, and a 128×32 I2C OLED on the front, using direct GPIO inputs with internal pull-ups.
Also, i auto routed it bc i dint have much time (thats why its so ugly).
